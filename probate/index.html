Common questions
What families and attorneys ask me most.
Do we need to go through probate to sell the property?
It depends on how the property was titled and your specific situation. I can give you general guidance, but your probate attorney will make the definitive determination. I work well with probate attorneys and can be a resource alongside theirs.
The property needs work — does it have to be perfect to sell?
Not necessarily. Depending on the market and price, selling in as-is condition can be the right strategy — particularly if the estate doesn't have funds for renovation. I'll give you an honest assessment of whether to invest in prep or sell as-is.
We're not ready yet — can we still talk?
Absolutely. An early conversation is almost always more valuable than a last-minute one. Understanding your options, the property's likely value, and what the process looks like costs nothing and could save significant time and money later.
How do we handle family members who disagree?
I've been in this situation many times. My role is to provide clear information, stay neutral, and let the decision-makers decide. I document everything and never put words in anyone's mouth. If there's a dispute, your attorney should be involved — and I'll work alongside them.
Do you work with out-of-state heirs?
Yes, frequently. Electronic signatures, virtual walkthroughs, and clear written communication allow me to work effectively with heirs who are not local. I can also coordinate property access and preparation on your behalf.
