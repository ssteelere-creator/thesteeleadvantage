The luxury process
Six steps built for properties that demand more.
01
Private consultation
Every luxury engagement begins with a private consultation — no assumptions, no templates. I understand the property, the timeline, the seller's priorities, and what a successful outcome looks like.
02
Precision valuation
Luxury properties require comparable analysis that goes beyond automated tools. I build valuations based on actual transaction experience in these specific markets — not algorithm estimates.
03
Pre-market exposure
Before the listing goes live, I introduce the property to my private buyer network and top-producing agent contacts who represent qualified buyers. The right buyer sometimes finds the property before it hits the MLS.
04
Premium marketing
Professional photography, aerial drone footage, video walkthrough, and a property-specific marketing plan that reaches the buyer profile this property requires. Generic marketing doesn't work at this level.
05
Qualified showings only
I pre-qualify buyers before scheduling showings. Your home is not open to curiosity seekers — only buyers who have demonstrated the financial capacity and genuine interest to make an offer.
06
Expert negotiation
At $750K+, every point of negotiation represents significant value. I protect your position on price, terms, contingencies, and closing timeline with the preparation and persistence this level of transaction demands.
