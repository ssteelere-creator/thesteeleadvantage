My story
Why I do this and how I got here.

I came up in real estate the hard way — working every part of the transaction, making mistakes, and learning what separates agents who build lasting practices from agents who flame out after two years. The difference isn't talent. It's systems, accountability, and genuine care about whether the client got the best possible outcome.

Southeastern Massachusetts and Rhode Island are my markets because they're markets I genuinely understand. Not just the data — the character. I know which Fall River neighborhoods are appreciating fastest and why. I know what Dartmouth's coastal pockets do when rates move. I know Tiverton's trajectory before it shows up in the headlines. That market intelligence is what I bring to every client conversation.

As CEO and Operating Principal at The Ponte Group, I'm also responsible for the performance of 14 agents and the culture that supports them. A team that performs at $125M+ annually doesn't happen by accident — it requires leadership, accountability systems, and a genuine investment in each agent's growth. Building that team has made me a better agent and a better advisor to every client I work with.

How I work
Four principles that don't bend under pressure.
01
Preparation before positioning
I prepare before I advise. Every client conversation is backed by data I've already pulled, analysis I've already done, and a position I've already thought through. I don't wing it with your biggest financial decision.
02
Honesty over comfort
I'll tell you things you might not want to hear — that the price you want to list at is too high, that the property you love has a problem, that the timing isn't right. That's not pessimism. That's the job.
03
Speed without sloppiness
Fast markets require fast responses — but speed without accuracy is just chaos. I respond same day, I move quickly on opportunities, and I never let urgency compromise the quality of what I deliver.
04
Accountable through closing
I don't disappear after the offer is accepted. The period between accepted offer and closing is where deals go wrong. I stay involved, I stay reachable, and I make sure nothing falls through the cracks.
