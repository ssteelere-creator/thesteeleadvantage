For homeowners in difficulty
You have more options than you think.
If you're facing missed payments, a notice of default, or a situation where the mortgage balance exceeds what the home is worth — please don't wait. Every option gets worse with time. Every option gets better when you engage early.
Short sale
If your home is worth less than you owe, a bank-approved short sale may allow you to sell the property and avoid foreclosure. I have experience negotiating with lenders on behalf of sellers in this situation.
Pre-foreclosure sale
Selling before foreclosure proceedings are complete protects your credit significantly more than a completed foreclosure. Even if you're in default, selling quickly may be possible and preserves far more of your financial future.
Understand your position
The first step is always a conversation. I can tell you what your home is worth today, what your options are, and what timeline you're working with — without any pressure or obligation. Call me directly.
