The Buying Process

How I work with buyers.

From first conversation to keys in hand — a clear, no-surprise process so you always know where you stand and what's next.

Step 01
Buyer Consultation

We start with a 30-minute conversation — in person, by phone, or video. I learn what you're looking for, what markets you're considering, your timeline, and your financing situation. This isn't a pitch. It's a strategy session.

Step 02
Financing & Pre-Approval

If you're not already pre-approved, I connect you with Michael Quin at NorthPointe Bank or Paul Salcone at Rate. Getting pre-approved before you search means you can move the moment you find the right property — not 72 hours later.

Step 03
Active Search & Access

I set up a custom search in Follow Up Boss so you see properties the moment they hit the market. I also pull off-market and pre-market opportunities through the team network and Zillow Preferred program.

Step 04
Showings & Analysis

I walk every property with you. I tell you what I see — the good, the potential problems, and what it's actually worth in today's market. I don't tell you what you want to hear. I tell you what you need to know.

Step 05
Offer & Negotiation

When you're ready to move, I build an offer strategy — price, terms, contingencies, escalation clauses when needed. The goal is to win the property at the right price, not just win it. I've been on both sides of these negotiations.

Step 06
Inspections to Closing

Once under contract, Tracy Pavao, our closing coordinator, manages the timeline. Inspections, repairs, title work, final walkthrough — every step is tracked and communicated. You're never left wondering what's happening or when.
