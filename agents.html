Who We're Looking For

We are selective.

We're not looking to fill seats. We're looking for agents who are ready to step into a structure that helps them produce more — and who will hold up their end of that structure.

◈
You're already producing

You don't need to be at 30 deals a year. But you need to be active. The sweet spot is agents doing 10–35 transactions who are hitting a ceiling they can't break through alone.

◈
You're coachable

You're willing to use the systems, follow the process, and take feedback. Agents who know everything already don't last long in a team environment — and they don't need to be here.

◈
You respond to clients

Same-day response is a non-negotiable for every agent on this team. If clients are waiting 48 hours to hear from you, that's a problem we'd need to solve before moving forward.

◈
You're licensed in MA and/or RI

We operate on both sides of the border. If you're only licensed in one state, we can talk about a path to adding the other — but active licensure is required.

This Is Probably Not the Right Fit If
You're looking for a place to get your license and figure it out
You want independence over infrastructure
You're not willing to use a CRM
You expect leads without accountability
You're not comfortable with consistent client communication
